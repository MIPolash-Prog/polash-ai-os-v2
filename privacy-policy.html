Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Overview

Polash AI OS is a personal site and productivity workspace built by Polash IT Solutions. This page explains what the site does — and does not — collect.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store or sell personal data. There are no accounts and no server-side database. Tasks, goals, notes, earnings, learning progress, saved tools and theme choice are stored only in your own browser's local storage and never sent to us.

Cookies and tracking

No advertising cookies, no third-party analytics scripts and no fingerprinting. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts; their own privacy terms apply to that request.

External links

The site links to third-party services such as Fiverr, Upwork, WhatsApp, YouTube, GitHub and the AI tools in the directory. When you open them, their own privacy policies apply.

Your control

You can export a JSON backup of your workspace at any time, and erase all locally stored data from the Workspace page. Erasing is permanent and happens entirely on your device.

Children's privacy

The site does not knowingly collect data from children or anyone else.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
