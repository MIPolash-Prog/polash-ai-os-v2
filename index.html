SEO · Saudi Arabia

Full SEO strategy for “Ajwa dates price in KSA”

Delights Oasis · delightsoasis.sa
The problem

A single keyword needed a complete plan before approval: on-page, off-page, local SEO and high-authority backlinks only. The site had thin content and technical clutter.

What I did
  • Audited with Semrush and Siteliner: 24% duplicate content, 41 broken internal links, 21 duplicate titles.
  • Chose the ranking page and wrote an on-page change sheet for titles, meta and content.
  • Compared the top 10 results, then planned local SEO, backlinks and a 6-month keyword map.
Delivered

A written strategy, on-page change sheet, competitor table, backlink sample sheet and 6-month keyword plan.

On-page SEO · 3 websites

On-page audits for three very different sites

10spotusa.com · jashimuddinpoly.edu.bd · jashimuddinmats.com
The problem

A US liquidation store, a polytechnic institute and a medical assistant training institute — each needing key pages matched to one clear search phrase.

What I did
  • Chose a target keyword and URL for each site, including local intent.
  • Wrote page titles within 65 characters and meta descriptions within 156.
  • Prepared matching content, image alt text and H1 headings for every page.
Delivered

One audit sheet with ready-to-paste titles, descriptions, content, alt text and H1 tags for all three websites.
